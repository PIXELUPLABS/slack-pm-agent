## 7. Handling New Ideas Mid-Project

Good projects evolve. When a new idea or direction emerges along the way, we'll be transparent about how to fold it in:

- **Included.** Small refinements that align with the approved direction — we absorb these into the current round.
- **Trade.** A new idea swaps in for something equivalent — same scope, same timeline, simply rebalanced.
- **Change request.** A new deliverable, new direction, or rework of approved work — we'll scope and quote it separately with a clear view of timeline impact, so the call sits with you with full information.

The intent is simple: keep the door open to good ideas, while protecting the commitments we've made to each other.
